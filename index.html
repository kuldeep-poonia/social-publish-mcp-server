User Guide

How To Use This Server.

Follow these 3 simple visual steps to publish content through AI assistants or directly from the web.

1

1. Connect in Claude / Cursor

In Claude Desktop, add URL https://social-mcp.duckdns.org/mcp (Streamable HTTP) or https://social-mcp.duckdns.org/mcp/sse (SSE). In Cursor, add it under Settings ➔ Features ➔ MCP.

2

2. Link Social Accounts (1-Click)

Click the Connect Account buttons above for Instagram, Twitter, or YouTube. Authorize the official Meta/Google popup once to store your AES-256 encrypted vault token.

3

3. Prompt & Publish Live

In Claude, Cursor, or Web Studio, simply tell your AI: "Post this photo to my Instagram with caption 'New Product Release 🚀'". The gateway publishes live in under 5 seconds!
